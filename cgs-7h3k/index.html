What We Do

Softwash roof & exterior cleaning

Low-pressure softwashing — gentle enough to protect your shingles and siding, strong enough to remove years of moss, algae and grime.

Roof Softwash Cleaning

Gentle, low-pressure cleaning that safely removes dirt, moss and algae from roofs without causing damage.

House Softwash Cleaning

Removes dirt, stains and algae from your home's exterior, leaving it spotless without risking any damage.

Gutter Cleanout

Removes debris to prevent flooding, roof damage and insect nests, keeping gutters working year-round.

Moss Removal

A gentle, low-pressure treatment that kills moss at the root on roofs, decks and driveways — no surface damage.

Meet the Owner

Ed Callaghan

Callaghan's Softwash is Chilliwack, BC's premier exterior cleaning company — gutter cleaning, roof cleaning, siding cleaning and softwashing.

"At 39, I needed a career change after 17 years working in the oilfield. I wanted an industry where I could help people and make them happy for a living."

Ed does everything himself — from setting up the quote to managing the billing — and reviews consistently mention him and his crew, including Dalton, by name.
