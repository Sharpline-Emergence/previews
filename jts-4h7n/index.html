FAQ

Common questions

Do you work after hours so my business isn’t disrupted?

Yes. We schedule around your operations, including evenings, nights and weekends.

Do I need to supply power or water?

No electricity is needed. Leave outdoor water taps on, close windows and doors, and unlock gates before we arrive.

How often should a commercial property be cleaned?

Regular cleaning needs less pressure and less time, is gentler on surfaces and usually costs less over time. We offer one-time cleans or routine service plans.

How fast will I get a quote?

We typically reply to quote requests within 24 hours.
