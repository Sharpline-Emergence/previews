Walk-in tubs

A tub for every bathroom, need and budget

Step in through a door instead of climbing over the edge. Our tubs come from a Canadian manufacturer in business since 1992, so parts and warranties are there when you need them.

Easiest to use

The Panama

Our simplest tub to get in and out of — an outward-swinging door with a 32″ wide entry, quick-filling taps and eight legs for stability.

Space-saving

The Fairmount

Fits anywhere a regular 5′ tub does, thanks to an all-aluminum door that swings inward. High-flow taps and stainless steel hoses.

Room to relax

The Oasis

Our newest and longest model at 60″, with a reclining backrest for a comfortable, neck-deep soak.

Six more models:
  • Zen — upright soak
  • Waterton — tight spaces
  • Kananaskis — roomy in-swing
  • Rio — 26″ compact
  • Grandeur — bariatric
  • So-Lo — budget-friendly
Accessible showers

Prefer a shower? No step required.

Safe showers for walkers, wheelchairs and anyone who’s had enough of stepping over a tub wall.

Phoenix barrier-free shower

A minimal ¾″ threshold, so wheelchairs and walkers roll straight in.

Low-step-in shower

Standard 10 mm barn-door style glass, with grab bars and a seat — ideal for seniors and anyone with limited mobility.

We also install:
  • Grab bars
  • Shower seats & guardrails
  • Shower inserts
  • Tub-to-shower conversions
  • Wheelchair-accessible showers
