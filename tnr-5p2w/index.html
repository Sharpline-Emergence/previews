Our story

Two honest men, then a family business, now a team the Valley trusts.

After immigrating from the Netherlands, the VandePol family has been roofing Chilliwack and Abbotsford since the mid-1980s. When founder Tony VandePol retired in 2016, his son Marcel and son-in-law Walter took over.

Today Top Notch runs several certified, uniformed crews — but it’s still a tight-knit, family-owned business that puts its hands and hearts into every roof.
