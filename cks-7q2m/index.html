Our story

Started to give people a better start

Carpet Shock was founded in Langley back in 2010 with a simple goal: do great work and give young people in the community a real trade and a path forward. That spirit is still how the team operates today — on time, straightforward pricing, and work they stand behind.

Reviewers keep mentioning the same thing: Doug shows up when he says he will, works hard, and treats the job like it's his own home.
