About us

Two brothers who grew up building things.

Justin and Jaren Jansen were born and raised in Chilliwack. As kids they spent countless hours designing and building tree forts, go-carts and motorbikes — and that passion turned into a trade.

Outlaw is a small, tight-knit shop. When you hire us, the owners themselves work on your project, and we’re proud to support local apprentices and Red Seal development.

  • Owner-operated
  • Born & raised in Chilliwack
  • 520,000+ sq ft of steelwork
